# pacs008-loader-mt103 v0.0.15

Released in lockstep with `pacs008` 0.0.15. The version skips 0.0.13 and
0.0.14 so the three packages of the suite share one number again.

## Parser changes

- A `:32A:` value date that does not exist (`000000`, 30 February) is
  rejected with a `ValueError` naming `:32A:` instead of becoming an
  impossible ISO date.
- A `:32A:` amount must be SWIFT `15d`: digits, at most one decimal
  comma, at most 15 characters. **Behaviour change:** an amount with a
  dot (`EUR1.234`) is now refused rather than read as a decimal point.

## Supply chain and CI

- `pacs008 >= 0.0.15, < 1`.
- Hash-pinned installs everywhere, actions pinned by commit SHA,
  read-only tokens by default.
- CodeQL, OpenSSF Scorecard, DCO, `pip-audit`, a complexity ratchet,
  Atheris and Hypothesis fuzzing, and a version-sources check.
- Releases carry SLSA provenance, sigstore signatures, PEP 740
  attestations and CycloneDX and SPDX SBOMs.

## Upgrading

`pip install --upgrade pacs008-loader-mt103`. If you feed amounts with a
dot as the decimal separator, convert them to SWIFT's comma first.
